# Privacy Policy — Flirt Dictionary

_Last updated: 18 November 2026_

Flirt Dictionary ("the app") is an offline dictionary of flirting, dating and romance vocabulary. This policy explains what information the app handles.

## Information stored only on your device
Your favourites, collections, recently viewed words and recent searches are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. We never receive them. They are deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall it. They may be included in your personal Android backup, which Google handles under your Google account settings.

## Information collected by third-party services
The app uses the following Google services, which collect some information automatically:

**Google AdMob (advertising).** Shows ads that fund the app. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (to estimate approximate location), device and app information, and ad interactions. It uses these to serve ads, which may be personalised, to measure them, and to prevent fraud. If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time under **⋮ → Ad privacy choices** on the Today screen. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

**Google Analytics for Firebase (usage statistics).** Collects anonymous usage information so we can improve the app: which screens and words are viewed, dictionary search terms, use of features such as favourites and sharing, app version, device model, OS version, approximate country, and an app-instance identifier. It does not collect your name, email address, contacts or messages.
Google's policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

## What we do not do
- We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
- We do not sell personal information.
- The app has no accounts and no user-generated content.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and is not designed for them.

## Data retention and deletion
Data held by Google Analytics is kept for the retention period configured in the Firebase project (default: 2 months for user-level data). To delete the analytics identifier stored on your device, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.
